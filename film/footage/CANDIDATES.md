# FixOdds footage research

Checked **5 November 2026**. Research only: **16 shortlisted clips, 1 conditional alternative, 4 excluded references**, with 21 downloaded preview images. **The brief is not fully fulfilled**: hook, bin, repaircafe and the second ewaste choice remain unresolved; the optional calendar slot is omitted. Excluded references are retained transparently for review, not recommended for use.

Every entry's licence was checked on its own clip page. Pexels pages explicitly show “License / Free” linked to the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/); the three Commons file pages explicitly specify [CC BY 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Pexels requires no credit. Commons credits are below, but those clips fail the visual requirement and are excluded.

Only small JPG preview images were downloaded. No video files were requested or downloaded, and no commits were made. HTML/API requests to Pexels, Pixabay, Mixkit and Commons returned HTTP 403; readable public pages were checked through the web reader, and CDN thumbnails were obtainable. No blocks were bypassed. Direct HD MP4 URLs were not exposed by that reader: `file_url` is `null`, not a guessed URL. Commons originals are WebM, not HD MP4.

Dimensions, duration and FPS are the original page's metadata, **not verified downloaded-rendition specifications**. Longer originals need a 5–30 s excerpt. `has_vertical=true` means the candidate itself is native vertical; `false` means no separate vertical rendition was verified, not proof none exists. Shop rank 3 provides a vertical option from the same shoot; an identical alternate take is not established.

All 21 preview images were visually inspected for subject, faces, text and branding. No moving clips were fetched, so complete-clip watermark, logo, text and screen-legibility checks remain open. Thumbnail observations and shot-fit limitations are stated explicitly. Factory footage shows automation but does not establish which device is manufactured; working kettles do not document an actual repair.

Review locally in [contact.html](contact.html). Machine-readable records: [candidates.json](candidates.json). `selection_status` separates shortlisted, conditional and excluded records; `eligible` is false for the last two.

## Exclusions and access limits

- Mixkit exact-fit clips were checked and rejected because their own pages specify **Mixkit Restricted License / personal use only**: [stressed over broken laptop](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/elderly-man-stressed-over-broken-laptop-9157/), [electronics assembly line](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/electronics-assembly-line-14785/), [electronics-manufacturing robot](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/robot-working-in-an-electronics-manufacturing-facility-47258/), [production-line robot](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/robot-working-in-a-production-line-47257/), [electronics factory](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/working-in-a-electronics-factory-22033/). Free category-page wording was not treated as clip permission.
- Pixabay repair, damaged-laptop and e-waste pages were readable and showed the Content License, but clip-specific preview/rendition information was not exposed by the reader. No Pixabay clip was promoted to the shortlist with guessed metadata or a related-media thumbnail.
- No suitable Coverr exact match was verified. Paid sites, YouTube downloads, AI-generated clips, ordinary plastic/paper disposal shots and unverified search results are not substitutes for the requested real device-disposal scene.
- “Shortlisted” means page-licensed research candidate with a relevant inspected preview, not final editorial clearance or a downloaded clip. Incidental people/brands must not be presented as endorsing FixOdds. No absolute absence of logos, text or identifiable people across an entire clip is claimed.
